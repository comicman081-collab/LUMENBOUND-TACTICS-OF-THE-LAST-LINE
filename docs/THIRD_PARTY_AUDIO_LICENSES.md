# Third-party battle audio

The battle SFX replacement uses only assets whose OpenGameArt source pages
declare Creative Commons CC0 1.0. Commercial use and modification are allowed;
attribution is not required. Credits are nevertheless kept below and in the
machine-readable audio lineage report.

- The Free Firearm Sound Library — Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras,
  Matthew Nanney — real firearm recordings.
- 25 CC0 bang / firework SFX — rubberduck — real fireworks, cannon-like bangs,
  and short shots.
- 100 CC0 metal and wood SFX — rubberduck — recorded impact materials.
- Various Sound Effects — Julie Damsgaard / Spring Spring / Spring Enterprises
  — bullet hit, player hit, and dull explosion selections.

Source-page URLs and the CC0 legal-code URL are recorded per source in
`data_source/audio_manifest.json`. Exact input/output hashes, byte sizes, and
rights declarations are written by `tools/audio/build_audio_pack.py` to
`reports/audio/AUDIO_LINEAGE_AUDIT.csv`. Card-to-layer assignments are written
to `reports/audio/CARD_START_AUDIO_AUDIT.csv`.
