# Character Design Bible

Each adult-woman character needs a distinct face shape, eye/brow language, hair silhouette, body proportion, costume shape language, material mix, weapon silhouette, palette and personality pose. Color swaps of one body or costume are prohibited.

CHR001 pilot language: teal-black-gold guardian, asymmetric layered hair, physically gripped shield and lantern flail, grounded defensive stance. CHR008 pilot language: pearl-lilac medic, white-indigo-mint equipment, injector wand and orbiting medical ring, lighter posture and precise hand language.

Required expressions are neutral, smile, serious, angry, sad and surprised. Face, neck, shoulder, scale and alpha bounding box must remain stable. Hands must contact grips; hair, body, costume and weapon intersections are hard failures.
