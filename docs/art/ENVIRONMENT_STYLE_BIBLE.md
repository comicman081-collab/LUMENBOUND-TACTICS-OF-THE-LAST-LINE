# Environment Style Bible

Story spaces are quiet relay chambers with portrait-safe negative space. Battle spaces require an explicit ground contact line, readable front/middle/back lanes and cool background values behind warmer character accents. Boss spaces use stronger scale cues and a distinct focal architecture.

Required conceptual layers are ground, near, mid, far and atmosphere. Flat gradients, blurred photos, primitive-only boxes and copied game backgrounds are prohibited. The R5 glass-rail battlefield and signal cathedral are independent internal images.
