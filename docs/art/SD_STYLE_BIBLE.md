# SD Style Bible

Target proportion is 2.8–3.2 heads with a large readable face, enlarged hands/feet and deliberately exaggerated role equipment. Players face lower-right at about 30 degrees; enemies deploy right and face lower-left. Canvas is 512×512 RGBA, foot anchor is `(0.50, 0.88)`, and runtime timing remains 12 fps with the immutable 80-frame state map.

The R5 pilot preserves idle 8, move 12, basic 8, normal 12, ultimate 18, hit 4, down 8 and victory 10. Damage is simulation-timed; frames are presentation. Full production requires articulated secondary motion and per-frame limb/weapon development beyond simple whole-key-pose interpolation.
