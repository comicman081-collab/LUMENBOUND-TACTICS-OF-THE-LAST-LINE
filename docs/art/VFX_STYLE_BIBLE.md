# VFX Style Bible

Every major effect needs a core, primary and secondary shape, energy/flame, spark/debris, impact, afterglow, dust/smoke and light response. Trails, shockwaves and ground contact are added where they improve impact.

CHR001 uses teal-gold ward geometry and cathedral-like shield motifs. CHR008 uses mint-lilac injector light, petals and constellation motifs. Effects must preserve their internal shapes at 1× and 3× and must not hide the HUD. A recolored basic attack is not an ultimate.
