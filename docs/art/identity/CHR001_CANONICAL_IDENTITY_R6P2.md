# CHR001 — 마에루 (DEV) / Canonical Identity R6P2

## Immutable gameplay identity

- Entity: `CHR001`
- Adult woman only; `GUARDIAN / FRONT / ENERGY / BARRIER / HEAVY`.
- Her battlefield language is protective advance, barrier anchoring, and a
  physical shield-and-lantern flail rather than ranged damage.

## Retained independent visual identity

- Hair: dense teal, high asymmetric ponytail with layered front locks and a
  gold geometric hair guard. It is neither a school-uniform silhouette nor an
  external character's hairstyle.
- Face: adult oval face, warm amber eyes, calm guarding expression; the pose
  must retain mature proportions and never read as childlike.
- Outfit: teal, ivory, charcoal, and gold segmented guardian armor with fitted
  dark underlayers, open but non-explicit combat panels, structured hip drapes,
  armored thigh-high boots, and deliberate material distinction between cloth,
  metal, crystal, and leather.
- Equipment: a large teal-and-gold physical barrier shield in the forward hand
  and a chained turquoise lantern flail in the rear hand. The shield is part of
  the pose and must never become a detached UI prop.
- Palette: deep teal / ivory / aged gold / graphite / cyan energy. This makes
  Maeru the guardian anchor of the team and avoids the red vanguard, blue
  rifle, purple compact, gold heavy, and specialist/medic palettes.

## Source and runtime contract

- Existing project-owned R6P2 output is retained without overwriting its
  original files. The SHA-256 of the Godot portrait/icon matches the original
  asset factory R6P2 manifest exactly.
- Lineage: `CHR001_MASTER_R6P2.blend`, `CHR001_PORTRAIT_R6P2.blend`, and
  `CHR001_ICON_R6P2.blend` under the internal asset factory; generator
  `premium-pilot-1.1.1`, Blender `4.5.11 LTS`, seed `20260818`.
