# CHR006 — 베라 (DEV) / Canonical Identity R1

## Immutable gameplay identity

- Entity: `CHR006`
- Adult female only; `SPECIALIST / MIDDLE / ENERGY / WARD / FOCUS`
- Story acquisition. Her battlefield language is relay analysis, enemy disruption,
  and precise control rather than direct firearm force.

## Independent visual identity

- Hair: frost-silver hair arranged as one heavy asymmetric side braid with a
  shorter loose crown section and a small brass signal clasp; never a rifle
  trooper ponytail silhouette.
- Face: calm adult oval face, pale teal-blue eyes, softly focused expression.
- Outfit: midnight-navy high-collar field bodysuit beneath a structured ivory
  navigator mantle with split panels, blue lining, brass fasteners, and layered
  translucent map-film tabs. The cut is adult and elegant while remaining
  non-explicit and practical for an expedition specialist.
- Focus: an original suspended cyan relay prism—an elongated faceted diamond
  held between three offset brass orbit arcs. It is a hand-guided field focus,
  not a gun, staff, shield, familiar, or an external-game symbol.
- Palette: ivory / midnight navy / frost silver / cyan with restrained brass
  accents. Its cool signal palette intentionally separates it from CHR003's
  cobalt rifle kit and CHR005's brass heavy cannon.
- Pose: a deliberate three-quarter standing navigator pose: one hand steadies
  the orbiting prism while the other anchors a compact field slate at the hip.
